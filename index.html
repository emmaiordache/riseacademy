What is RISE Academy?

Too many motivated young people across Eastern Europe have ideas about how to improve their schools, communities, and countries, but no clear structure to act on them. RISE fills that gap: we bring high school students together for workshops, mentorship, and a hands-on project incubator that takes real civic ideas from concept to reality.

Understanding civic systems and democratic institutions

Building community projects that address real local problems

Developing leadership, public speaking, and communication skills

Passion Project Incubator: coaching for selected civic initiatives

This Summer: AGORA Civic Leadership & Action Lab

A free, full-day, in-person program for students who want to turn a civic idea into a real initiative, supported by Projects for Peace and held in multiple cities across Eastern Europe.

Through short presentations, hands-on team exercises, and direct feedback from mentors, participants define a community problem, map the people and resources needed to address it, and build and pitch a concrete project. Winning teams receive prizes and are invited to a follow-up online bootcamp on September 5 and 6 for continued support in developing their project.

Reflect & Learn

We open with a reflection on peacebuilding, then dive into participatory democracy, global citizenship, and civic leadership through a hands-on exercise.

Engage Stakeholders & Funding

Participants build a stakeholder map for their chosen problem: potential partners, funders, and institutions, along with an estimated budget.

Build Your Civic Story

Mentors share their own experience, and participants craft their own civic story to weave into their final pitch.

Pitch, Win & Keep Building

Teams pitch their projects to judges. Winners receive prizes and continued support through our online bootcamp.

Spots are limited in each city. Head to our Linktree to see all the dates and locations and register for this summer's Lab.

Who should apply

You don't need experience. You need curiosity and drive.

High school students (grades 9–12)

Any student currently enrolled in high school across Eastern Europe. Our lab runs across different cities, we come to you.

People who care about their community

You don't need a polished project idea, just genuine interest in making things better for the people around you.

Future leaders of any field

Whether you're going into medicine, engineering, law, or the arts, civic leadership is relevant to every path.

Students who want to build, not just listen

RISE is hands-on. If you want to sit in a lecture hall and take notes, this isn't for you. If you want to do the work, welcome.
