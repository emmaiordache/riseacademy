What is RISE Academy?

Too many motivated young Romanians have ideas about how to improve their schools, communities, and country — but no structure to act on them. RISE fills that gap. We bring high school students together across Romania for workshops, mentorship, and a hands-on project accelerator that takes real civic ideas from concept to reality.

Understanding civic systems and democratic institutions

Building community projects that address real local problems

Developing leadership, public speaking, and communication skills

Passion Project Accelerator — coaching for selected civic initiatives

From where you are to where you want to be

Every participant starts somewhere. We help you get to where you want to go.

Who should apply

You don't need experience. You need curiosity and drive.

High school students (grades 9–12)

Any student currently enrolled in a Romanian high school. Applications are open across cities — we come to you.

People who care about their community

You don't need a polished project idea — just genuine interest in making things better for the people around you.

Future leaders of any field

Whether you're going into medicine, engineering, law, or the arts — civic leadership is relevant to every path.

Students who want to build, not just listen

RISE is hands-on. If you want to sit in a lecture hall and take notes, this isn't for you. If you want to do the work — welcome.
